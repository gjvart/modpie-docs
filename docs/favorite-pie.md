# Favorites Preset Pie

<div className="hero-badge-container">
  <span className="badge badge--primary">Modpie Plus Exclusive</span>
</div>

:::info 9.8.0+ Streamlined Access
Starting in **Modpie Plus 9.8.0**, saved presets and stack templates are integrated directly into the main **Alt + M** radial menu via the **Presets & Stacks** slot (SE) and panel header buttons. This eliminates hotkey collisions while keeping your favorites accessible in a single flick. For earlier builds or custom keymaps, a dedicated preset pie shortcut (<kbd>Shift + Alt + M</kbd>) is also supported.
:::

Modpie Plus gives you lightning-fast radial access to your favorite modifier presets directly under your cursor.

## 1. Muscle-Memory Radial Presets

- **Up to 8 Favorites**: Holds up to 8 of your hand-picked favorite modifier presets arranged radially.
- **Direction Stability**: Favourites maintain their exact compass direction as you add more items, so your muscle memory never shifts.
- **Search on Empty Slots**: The first unoccupied slot in the pie displays **Search Presets**, giving you instant access to your complete library without opening menus.

---

## 2. Managing Favorites

- To mark any preset as a favorite, click the heart or star icon in the Presets Library manager or in **Preferences ▸ Plus**.
- Rebind the shortcut (<kbd>Shift + Alt + M</kbd>) anytime in **Edit ▸ Preferences ▸ Add-ons ▸ Modpie ▸ Keymap**.
