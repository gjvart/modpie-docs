# Favorites Preset Pie

<div className="hero-badge-container">

Every 3D artist has a core set of go-to modifier configurations they reach for again and again—such as a 3-segment bevel for hard-surface edges, a weighted normal pass for clean shading, or a tuned solidify thickness.

Modpie Plus gives you lightning-fast radial access to your favorite modifier presets directly under your mouse cursor, letting you apply your favorite setups with a quick flick.

## Two Ways to Access Your Favorites

Modpie Plus integrates your favorites seamlessly across both the main pie and a dedicated secondary pie:

### 1. In the Main Radial Pie (<kbd>Alt + M</kbd>)
Whenever you open the main Modpie radial menu (<kbd>Alt + M</kbd>), the **Presets & Stacks** slot (located on South-East by default) displays your hand-picked favorites right at the top of the list. A single gesture lets you pick from your most-used recipes without cluttering your keyboard shortcuts.

### 2. The Dedicated Favorites Pie (<kbd>Shift + Alt + M</kbd>)
For pure muscle memory, Modpie Plus includes a secondary **8-way radial pie menu** dedicated exclusively to your starred favorites:
- **Up to 8 Favorites**: Holds up to 8 of your top modifier presets arranged around the 8 compass directions.
- **Direction Stability**: Each preset stays locked to its compass direction as you add more favorites, so your muscle memory never shifts.
- **Search on Open Slots**: If you have fewer than 8 favorites assigned, the first open slot automatically displays **Search Presets**, giving you instant access to your entire preset library with zero menu digging.

## How to Star & Manage Favorites

Marking a preset as a favorite takes just one click:

1. Open the **Presets** dropdown in the Modpie Viewport Panel (or open **Preferences ▸ Plus ▸ Presets Library**).
2. Click the **Star** (<kbd>⭐</kbd>) icon next to any saved modifier preset.
3. The preset immediately appears in your **Favourites** list, in the **Presets & Stacks** slot of the main pie menu, and in the dedicated **Favorites Pie** (<kbd>Shift + Alt + M</kbd>).
4. Click the star icon again at any time to unstar it.

---

## Customizing the Shortcut

If you prefer a different hotkey for the dedicated Favorites Pie:
- Go to **Edit ▸ Preferences ▸ Add-ons ▸ Modpie ▸ Shortcuts**.
- Locate **Favorites Preset Pie (Plus)** and assign any key combination that fits your setup.
